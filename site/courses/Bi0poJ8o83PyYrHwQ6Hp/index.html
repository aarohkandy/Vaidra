AI Automation for Everyday Work

Learn to hand the repetitive parts of your schoolwork and everyday tasks to AI, and keep the decisions for yourself. This beginner course explains what automation really means, how to tell repeated work from judgment work, and why language models changed what can be automated. You will learn to write prompts as clear instructions, give the model context and examples, and ask for output you can actually use. Then you will build real automations for flashcards, writing and summarizing, spreadsheets and data, and chain steps together into a workflow. Three quizzes check your understanding along the way. No coding needed.

Yuvin Agrawal
