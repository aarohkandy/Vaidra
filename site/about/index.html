About Us

Our Mission

Vaidra is dedicated to making education accessible to all by bridging the language gap in learning. We provide free educational audiobooks in regional Indian languages, empowering learners who may face barriers with traditional educational resources.

Our goal is to democratize knowledge and create equal opportunities for learning, regardless of language proficiency or literacy levels.

Vaidra was founded in 2024 in Bellevue, Washington, by Venkata Anirudh Devireddy, its Founder and CEO. It is registered as Vaidra Education, a Washington State nonprofit corporation.

Our School Partners

Reaching a network of 60,000+ students across 450+ schools in India, bringing accessible education in their mother tongue.

Siddhartha Institute of Engineering and Technology

Affiliated with JNTU Hyderabad and part of the Siddhartha Group of Institutions (est. 1994), SIET is an AICTE-approved, NBA-accredited engineering college near Hyderabad. Its mission to develop socially conscious technocrats aligns closely with Vaidra's goal of making quality education accessible to all.

Visit website →

Siddharth Institute of Engineering & Technology (SIETK)

Autonomous engineering college in Puttur, Andhra Pradesh (est. 2001), permanently affiliated with JNTUA and AICTE-approved. NAAC A+ (Cycle-II) and NBA-accredited, SIETK offers UG, PG, and diploma programs under Siddharth Institutions, including the former SISTK campus, expanding access to quality technical education across the region.

Visit website →

Kanchikacherla, Andhra Pradesh

Zilla Parishad High School, Kanchikacherla

A government Zilla Parishad secondary school serving learners across the Kanchikacherla region. This partnership extends Vaidra's mother-tongue courses and audiobooks into a public-school classroom, where English-first digital tools often fall short, so students can learn science, skills, and self-improvement in languages they already speak.

Chennai, Tamil Nadu

Sevalaya

A Chennai nonprofit (est. 1988) that runs a free school from kindergarten to Class 12 at Kasuva, Tiruvallur, along with after-school study centres in 50+ locations, community colleges, and homes for children and elders. The partnership brings Vaidra's mother-tongue courses and audiobooks to Sevalaya students across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh.

Visit website →

Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh

Rural Development Trust (RDT)

Founded in 1969 by Vicente Ferrer, RDT works in 3,900+ villages across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Its supplementary schools give children in government schools extra tuition close to home, and its inclusive schools and rehabilitation centres support children with disabilities. The partnership brings Vaidra's mother-tongue courses and audiobooks to RDT's network of 54,000+ students.
