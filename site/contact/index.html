Apply to be a Speaker!

Join our mission as a volunteer speaker! Help us translate and narrate English audiobooks into regional Indian languages, making education accessible to millions of learners in their mother tongue.

What do speakers do?

  • Translate educational content from English to regional Indian languages
  • Record high-quality audio narrations of translated books
  • Help review and improve existing translations
  • Collaborate with our team to ensure cultural accuracy and accessibility
  • Make a lasting impact on education in your community
Apply to volunteer
